Our Learning Methodology

A structured approach to Japanese language mastery

Before Class

Pre-class Preparation

Study materials and preview videos are shared 24 hours before each class. Students review vocabulary and basic concepts to maximize in-class learning.

  • PDF notes and worksheets
  • Preview videos (5-10 minutes)
  • Quick pre-quiz to assess knowledge
During Class

Interactive Live Session

90-minute live class with direct interaction. Students participate actively through speaking, writing, and Q&A.

  • Real-time conversation practice
  • Guided writing exercises
  • Instant doubt clearing
  • Pair/group activities in breakout rooms
After Class

Post-class Practice

Reinforce learning with homework, assignments, and access to recorded sessions.

  • Class recording within 24h
  • Homework assignments
  • Speaking practice submission
  • Teacher feedback on work
Weekly

Weekly Review & Assessment

End-of-week review session to consolidate learning and identify areas needing improvement.

  • Weekly quiz
  • Progress tracking
  • Individual feedback session

Class Rules & Guidelines

Punctuality

  • Join class 5 minutes before start time
  • Late entries may miss important instructions
  • Inform sensei in advance if delayed
  • 3 late arrivals = 1 absence

Camera & Audio

  • Camera must be ON during class
  • Mute when not speaking
  • Use headset for better audio
  • Find quiet space for class

Homework & Participation

  • Complete assignments on time
  • Active participation required
  • Ask questions freely
  • Respect all classmates

Exam FAQs

Level 1 quizzes are auto-graded instantly with results shown immediately. Level 2 exams are a combination of auto-graded sections and teacher-reviewed portions (like writing and speaking). Detailed feedback is provided within 48 hours.

For weekly quizzes, 80% is considered passing. For monthly tests and JLPT mocks, we follow the official JLPT passing criteria which varies by level but typically requires around 60-70% overall with no section scoring below 30%.

Yes! You can retake Level 1 quizzes unlimited times for practice. Level 2 exams can be retaken once per month. Mock tests can be taken as many times as needed with different question sets each time.

Yes, we guide students through the JLPT registration process, provide information about test centers in Bangladesh, and help with form filling. We also organize group registrations for our students.

Once you log in to your student dashboard, you'll find a complete history of all your exams, scores, and teacher feedback. You can track your progress over time and identify areas for improvement.
